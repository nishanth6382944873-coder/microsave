📌 SHG Core Business Principles

  • Pooled Capital: All members make periodic savings. The pooled savings form the group lending fund.
  • Internal Lending: Loans are given exclusively from the available pooled balance.
  • Available Pool Rule: Available Pool = Total Contributions - Total Outstanding Loans
  • Single Active Loan Rule: A member with an active unpaid loan cannot take another loan.
  • Auto-Closure: When full loan repayment is recorded, status is automatically updated to CLOSED.

⚡ Quick Management Actions

👥 Register New Member: Add a woman member to the SHG 💰 Record Savings: Log weekly deposit for a member 📑 Disburse Internal Loan: Grant loan against available pool 🔄 Record Repayment: Reduce outstanding loan balance
